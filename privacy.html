DriveLine: Barista Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

DriveLine: Barista Rush ("the app") is a free iOS game developed by Rico Xu ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in. I do not run servers that collect your gameplay data.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • Voice input is processed on your device by Apple's speech recognition; your audio is never sent to me.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which collects device data as described below.
  • The app offers optional in-app purchases, processed entirely by Apple.

1. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — only if you grant the App Tracking Transparency permission — and the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV);
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address);
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, taps);
  • Device and app performance data used to serve and measure ads.

On first launch you will see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads still work but are less personalized, and the IDFA is not shared. Your choice is honored and can be changed any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising partner disclosures.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

You may optionally speak customer orders instead of typing them. When you press the Speak button:

  • Audio is captured by the microphone and transcribed on your device using Apple's Speech framework (SFSpeechRecognizer, on-device mode) and Apple's on-device neural voices.
  • Audio recordings and transcripts are not stored, uploaded, or shared by the app. They exist only in memory while the order is processed.

Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are both optional — every order can also be entered by tapping the on-screen ticket. You can revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings; the game remains fully playable.

3. Voice downloads

On first launch the app downloads additional on-device text-to-speech voice data from publicly hosted model repositories (Hugging Face) so customer voices can be synthesized locally. This download contains no personal data in either direction beyond what any HTTPS request reveals (IP address at the network level, handled by the repository host).

4. In-app purchases

The app offers optional purchases (lives and boosters) through Apple's App Store / StoreKit. Payment is processed entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details. Purchase history is used only to unlock what you bought and to support "Restore Purchases".

5. Game data stored on your device

Progress, settings, lives/booster balances, and achievement state are stored locally on your device (UserDefaults and app sandbox files). Deleting the app deletes this data. None of it is transmitted to me.

6. What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of my own;
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers;
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, photos, or health data;
  • No user-generated content, chat, or social features that expose you to other players.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience game rated 4+. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are served through Google AdMob in a manner consistent with Apple's App Store requirements for the 4+ rating.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
