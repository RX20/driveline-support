Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Customers drive up and speak their order through the intercom. Remember it, then ring it up on the register before the car times out. Easy mode is the gentlest way to learn, and Training on the main menu is a free guided practice round — no lives at stake. A full How to Play tutorial is also on the main menu.

How do I speak orders instead of tapping?

Tap and hold the Speak button while entering an order. The first time, iOS will ask for Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions — both are optional, and typing always works. Recognition happens entirely on your device.

My purchase didn't show up / I reinstalled the app

Open the Shop (cart icon on the main menu) and tap Restore Purchases. Any non-consumable purchases tied to your Apple ID will be restored.

The customer voices sound robotic or silent

On first launch the app downloads extra on-device voice data — connect to Wi-Fi and relaunch. If a voice still falls back to the system voice, it will keep improving as the downloads complete. No personal data is involved; see the Privacy Policy.

How do lives work?

Lives refill over time, and you can refill instantly or grab boosters in the Shop. Training mode never costs lives.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy. The app has no accounts; progress stays on your device; voice input is processed on-device; ads are served by Google AdMob with your App Tracking Transparency choice honored.
